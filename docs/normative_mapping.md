# Regulatory context

`CR-001` is a reviewed contextual source concerning cash operations. It is not an automatic legal predicate or an internal policy of the reviewed organization.

The official [National Bank communication](https://nationalbank.kz/ru/news/informacionnye-soobshcheniya/17232) references Board Resolution No. 231 dated 29 November 2019 and describes cash receipt/issuance as banking cash operations. This supports the subject context only. A current complete consolidated provision and event-specific applicability must be reviewed before claiming a breached obligation.

The runtime registry caches source title, issuer, URL, section precision, supported proposition, applicability and limitations. `verified` refers to the source and stated contextual proposition; `enabled_for_legal_claim` remains false. A link does not prove noncompliance.

The model’s generic visual signal does not identify intent, authority, identity, cash amount, accounting correctness or off-camera behavior. Human review must establish the visible action, complete sequence, relevant exceptions, and an applicable obligation before reaching a conclusion. Source images and timestamps alone cannot establish those missing facts.

Future event mappings need a reviewed type, complete interval annotations, visible start/end conditions, counterexamples, and an exact applicable provision where available. Other organizations’ procedures are supporting material only unless their applicability is established.
